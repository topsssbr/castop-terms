Castop - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Castop collects, uses, and protects information when users access or interact with the platform.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, Castop may process:

  • Basic TikTok account information made available through authorized permissions.
  • Account identifiers and authorization information required to connect to TikTok.
  • Content created or selected by users, including captions, images, and publication details.
  • Information necessary to manage content approval and publishing workflows.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate Castop, organize gaming and technology news, prepare content for publication, manage user approvals, and provide authorized TikTok publishing features.

4. Data Storage and Security

Castop uses a self-hosted automation environment to process and manage its workflows. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are applied to protect stored information and access credentials.

5. Sharing of Information

Castop does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party platforms when necessary to provide requested features, including TikTok, in accordance with their applicable policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as necessary for the operation of the service and its related workflows. Users may request information about their data or request deletion through the contact channel provided below.

7. Third-Party Services

Castop may interact with third-party services, including TikTok. Their privacy policies and terms also apply to information processed by those services.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be published on this page.
